1. About HydroTrack

HydroTrack is a hydration-tracking web application designed to help users record their water intake, set daily hydration goals, monitor progress, and build consistent hydration habits.

HydroTrack is currently developed as a portfolio and demonstration project by Muhammad Abdullah Khan.

As the application develops, its technical infrastructure and data-storage methods may change. This Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features available in the version of HydroTrack you use, the application may process the following categories of information.

Account Information

When you create an account, HydroTrack may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials

Passwords should be handled through appropriate authentication mechanisms and should not be displayed publicly.

Hydration Information

When using the hydration tracker, you may provide or generate information such as:

  • Daily water intake
  • Daily hydration goal
  • Preferred serving size
  • Measurement-unit preferences
  • Daily progress
  • Weekly hydration activity
  • Goal-completion history
  • Hydration streaks

This information is used to provide HydroTrack's tracking and progress features.

Application Preferences

HydroTrack may store preferences such as:

  • Selected measurement unit
  • Daily hydration target
  • Serving size
  • Interface preferences
  • Other application settings

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by HydroTrack may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide hydration-tracking functionality.
  • Save daily water-intake records.
  • Calculate hydration progress.
  • Display weekly progress and summaries.
  • Calculate hydration streaks and goal completion.
  • Remember user preferences and settings.
  • Maintain and improve application functionality.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Protect the application against misuse.

HydroTrack does not use hydration information to provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

4. Authentication Information

HydroTrack may require authentication to provide access to personalized application features.

When production-style authentication is implemented, passwords should be processed using secure password-handling practices rather than stored as readable plain text.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should avoid sharing their passwords with others.

Because HydroTrack is currently a development and portfolio project, users should not reuse passwords from important personal, financial, educational, or professional accounts.

5. How Information Is Stored

HydroTrack's storage methods may depend on the version of the application.

Development or demonstration versions may use browser-based storage such as:

  • localStorage
  • sessionStorage

More advanced versions may use a backend application and database to store account information, hydration records, settings, and other application data.

The exact infrastructure may change as HydroTrack develops.

6. Local Browser Storage

HydroTrack may use browser storage to maintain information such as:

  • Login/session state
  • Hydration progress
  • Application preferences
  • Settings

Information stored locally may remain on your device until it is removed by the application, cleared through browser settings, or otherwise deleted.

Clearing your browser's storage may cause locally stored HydroTrack information to be permanently lost.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HydroTrack may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies when necessary to maintain sessions, preferences, security, or application functionality.

HydroTrack does not currently claim to use advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies.

If analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated to explain their use.

8. How Information Is Shared

HydroTrack does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate infrastructure or services used by the application.
  • Protect the security and integrity of HydroTrack.
  • Investigate misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.
  • Protect the rights or safety of users or others.

If third-party service providers are introduced for hosting, databases, authentication, analytics, or other functionality, this Privacy Policy should be updated where appropriate.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures should be used to protect information handled by HydroTrack.

However, no website, database, network, or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through HydroTrack.

HydroTrack should not be used to store information such as:

  • Financial account details
  • Government identification numbers
  • Medical records
  • Highly confidential personal information

10. Health Information and Medical Disclaimer

HydroTrack records hydration-related information for personal tracking purposes.

HydroTrack is not a medical service or medical device, and hydration information entered into or calculated by the application is not intended to replace professional medical advice.

Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional regarding individual hydration requirements, medical conditions, or health concerns.

11. Data Accuracy

HydroTrack relies partly on information entered by users.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information such as water intake and hydration goals is entered accurately.

Calculated statistics, progress percentages, streaks, and weekly summaries may depend on the accuracy of this information.

12. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide HydroTrack's functionality or satisfy applicable technical, security, or legal requirements.

For locally stored information, retention may depend on the user's browser and device settings.

When backend account storage is introduced, specific retention and deletion practices may be updated in this Privacy Policy.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the features available, users may be able to:

  • Update hydration goals.
  • Change application preferences.
  • Reset hydration progress.
  • Sign out of their account.
  • Clear locally stored application information.
  • Request changes to or deletion of account information where supported.

Additional privacy controls may be introduced as HydroTrack develops.

14. Account and Data Deletion

The current demonstration version may allow certain locally stored information to be removed by resetting application data or clearing browser storage.

When persistent backend accounts are implemented, HydroTrack should provide an appropriate mechanism for requesting or performing account and associated data deletion.

Deleting an account may result in permanent loss of associated hydration history and settings.

15. Children's Privacy

HydroTrack is not specifically designed to knowingly collect personal information from young children.

If future versions of HydroTrack are intentionally offered to children or collect information from them, additional consent, privacy, and legal requirements may apply and this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

16. Third-Party Links

HydroTrack may contain links to external websites or services.

HydroTrack is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of independent third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

HydroTrack may update this Privacy Policy as the application develops, particularly when new features, databases, authentication systems, analytics, hosting providers, or other services are introduced.

When the policy is updated, the Last Updated date at the top of this page should also be changed.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
